Global Renewable Energy Growth Reaches New Milestone

Published on October 8, 2026 • By Environmental Desk

Renewable power capacity additions surged by over 45% worldwide this year, led primarily by substantial solar photovoltaic installations and offshore wind developments across Asia and Europe.

According to the International Energy Council, renewable technologies now represent over 30% of total electricity generation capacity globally. Investment in battery storage technologies also tripled over the last eighteen months, addressing grid balancing challenges.

Analysts anticipate that sustained policy commitments and declining equipment manufacturing costs will accelerate this transition further over the next decade.
